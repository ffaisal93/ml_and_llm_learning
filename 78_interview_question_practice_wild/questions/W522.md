## Question

Tell me about a recent piece of news or article that made you excited to apply for this role and/or program at BlackRock.

## Worked solution

### Connect a verified development to the actual role

The interviewer is testing whether your interest has substance. Choose one dated announcement you have read, explain the technical/business implication, then connect it to work you genuinely want to do. The target role and your project history are not supplied, so the personal connection must be completed with your real experience.

A concrete historical example is the December 1, 2025 announcement that BlackRock and AWS would offer Aladdin clients an additional cloud-hosting option. Treat this as a dated practice example, not automatically “recent” at a later interview. Verify a newer development and its implementation status before using that adjective. The announcement is available in the [official release](https://press.aboutamazon.com/aws/2025/12/blackrock-partners-with-aws-to-deliver-aladdin-on-secure-scalable-cloud-infrastructure).

### Study response

“I was interested in the announcement about bringing Aladdin to AWS because it connects financial analytics with a difficult engineering problem: giving clients deployment flexibility while preserving a consistent, reliable platform. The part that interests me is the work behind that promise—data correctness, secure access, reproducible calculations and observability across environments. For a [specific role], I would be excited to contribute to [relevant responsibility]. In my [real project], I worked on [actual related challenge], and that made me want to solve similar problems with demanding users and clear reliability requirements.”

This is a **practice structure**, not a statement that you completed the bracketed experience. Use a cloud-platform angle for infrastructure roles, reproducible risk-data pipelines for data roles, or evaluated analytics workflows for ML roles. Do not assert that an announcement proves every migration, performance target or AI capability already exists.

### Make it more than praise

Add one thoughtful question: “How does the team validate that the same portfolio input gives consistent results across supported environments?” or “Which reliability metric matters most to users of this component?” Explain one trade-off, such as portability versus provider-specific optimization. Keep the answer around a minute, leaving room to discuss the role. Avoid reciting assets-under-management figures or market headlines unrelated to the job; one understood development is enough.
