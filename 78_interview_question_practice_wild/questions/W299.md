## Question

<pre class="question-text" style="white-space:pre-wrap;font-family:inherit;line-height:1.55">Expected Value of Reciprocal of Trials Until First Six

Given a fair six-sided die, it is rolled n times until the number 6 appears for the first time. Calculate the expected value of 1/n.

Example

Input

1</pre>

## Worked solution

1. Find E[1/N] when N is the trial of the first six. This is the expectation of a reciprocal geometric random variable, not the reciprocal of its expectation.
2. Let p=1/6 and q=1-p, so P(N=n)=p*q^(n-1).
3. Sum E[1/N]=(p/q)*sum(q^n/n) from n=1 to infinity.
4. Use sum(q^n/n)=-log(1-q)=-log(p), giving -p*log(p)/q.

### Small example / sanity check

For a fair die, E[1/N]=log(6)/5, approximately 0.35835; 1/E[N]=1/6 is different.

### Common trap

Moving the reciprocal outside the expectation.
