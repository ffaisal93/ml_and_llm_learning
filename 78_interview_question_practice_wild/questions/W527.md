## Question

<pre class="question-text" style="white-space:pre-wrap;font-family:inherit;line-height:1.55">Why do you want to change your job in less than a year?</pre>

## Worked solution

1. Present your background in ninety seconds. Connect your real experience to the role through a small number of relevant capabilities and outcomes.
2. Start with your current focus and the kinds of problems you solve.
3. Choose one or two concrete projects, naming your role, difficult decision and measured result.
4. Finish with why the target work fits your interests and what you want to contribute, without inventing credentials or metrics.

### Apply the exact contract

Answer the specific question directly using truthful details: motivation connects your interests to the work; a job-change answer explains the decision without disparaging others; a timeline answer states real deadlines and availability. For active contacts, share only what you are comfortable disclosing and never invent offers, company names or commitments. For a news-based motivation prompt, verify the relevant fact before using it; no specific current headline is supplied here.

### Small example / sanity check

An MLE introduction can connect data-quality work, reliable serving and one measured deployment result instead of listing every library used.

### Common trap

Reciting the entire resume chronologically or making claims you cannot defend under follow-up.
