## Question

<pre class="question-text" style="white-space:pre-wrap;font-family:inherit;line-height:1.55">How do you manage your team member&#x27;s stress?</pre>

## Worked solution

1. Handle harsh technical feedback. Separate the useful content of feedback from its delivery and verify what needs to change.
2. Listen, summarize the specific concern and ask for concrete examples when necessary.
3. Check the evidence, acknowledge valid mistakes and propose a bounded correction with a follow-up date.
4. If disagreement remains, explain it respectfully using the shared goal and evidence, and seek the appropriate resolution channel.

### Apply the exact contract

Ask privately about workload and constraints, listen without diagnosing, and agree on a realistic reprioritization or support plan. Escalate urgent safety or workplace-support needs through the appropriate channel. Follow up on workload and progress while protecting the colleague’s privacy; do not promise an outcome outside your authority.

### Small example / sanity check

Feedback that a report “is not reproducible” can lead to adding fixed configs, dependency notes and a clean-run command.

### Common trap

Claiming you always accept every criticism or always prove the critic wrong.
