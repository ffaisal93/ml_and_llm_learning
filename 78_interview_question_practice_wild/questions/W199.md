## Question

<pre class="question-text" style="white-space:pre-wrap;font-family:inherit;line-height:1.55">IBM intern and co-op follow-up interviews can be non-coding behavioural/resume rounds. One video-style competency screen gives one minute to prepare and three minutes to answer each question; another live interview focuses on motivation, missed deadlines, and project choices.

Requirements

Video competency format:

Five questions.
One minute preparation per question.
Three minutes to answer each question.

Example prompts:

Tell us about a time a team project went very well. What part did you play, and how did you help the team succeed?
Think of a time when preparation paid off. What were you working on?

Live co-op / SDE interview prompts:

Biggest strength.
A time you missed a deadline.
Why IBM?
Why this role?
Resume project follow-ups: why the project, how you built it, and what technologies you used.
Notes
The live format may run 40-60 minutes with two interviewers and no coding.
Resume follow-ups are usually not deep architecture probes; they focus on motivation, ownership, and whether the listed stack is real.</pre>

## Worked solution

1. Explain your most technically complex ML project. A strong answer demonstrates decisions and evidence, not a list of technologies.
2. State the user problem, constraints and your own contribution separately from the team's work.
3. Describe the data, baseline, hardest technical failure, alternatives considered and why the chosen approach fit.
4. Quantify the result using a valid comparison, explain limitations and say what you would change next. Use real experiences rather than invented metrics.
5. Discuss the data strategy behind an ML project. Data choices can explain more of a project's outcome than the architecture name.
6. Describe collection, permissions, label generation, cleaning and the intended deployment distribution.
7. Explain split design, duplicates, hard examples, reviewer quality and versioning.
8. Connect one data intervention to a controlled result and identify unresolved gaps.

### Small example / sanity check

“I reduced p95 latency from 800 to 500 ms on a matched workload while keeping task accuracy within the predefined margin” states both benefit and guardrail.

### Common trap

Saying “we built an LLM system” without explaining your choices or how success was measured.
