## Worked solution

### Solution outline

#### Why are Q, K and V separate projections?

Attention is a learned lookup: a query says what to find, a key says what can be matched, and a value carries what to copy.

1. Write $Q=XW_Q$, $K=XW_K$ and $V=XW_V$, with X shaped (B,T,d_model).
2. Compute scores $QK^\top$, normalize each query's row over keys, then mix the value vectors.
3. Separate Q and K allow asymmetric relevance. Separate V lets the features used for matching differ from the features transmitted; the output projection mixes heads.

#### Why divide attention logits by the square root of the head dimension?

It controls the initial scale of the dot products so softmax is less likely to become nearly one-hot before learning.

1. Assume independent, zero-mean, unit-variance query and key coordinates. Each product has variance one.
2. A sum of d_k such products has variance d_k and standard deviation sqrt(d_k).
3. Divide by sqrt(d_k), then subtract the row maximum before exponentiation. The divisor controls scale; max subtraction prevents overflow. These are different mechanisms.

#### Trace every shape in multi-head self-attention.

Heads are parallel attention computations over smaller feature spaces; they do not each attend to a disjoint piece of the sequence.

1. Project X:(B,T,d) into Q,K,V:(B,T,H*d_h). Reshape and transpose to (B,H,T,d_h).
2. Multiply Q by K transposed on its last two axes to obtain (B,H,T,T). Softmax over the final key axis.
3. Multiply by V to obtain (B,H,T,d_h), transpose to (B,T,H,d_h), concatenate to (B,T,H*d_h), and apply Wo to return to (B,T,d).

#### Why do Transformers need positional information, and what does RoPE do?

Without position information, ordinary self-attention cannot reliably distinguish token order. RoPE puts position-dependent rotations into queries and keys.

1. Split each head vector into coordinate pairs and rotate each pair by its position times a frequency.
2. Use rotated Q and K in the attention dot product. The relative rotation depends on the difference between their positions.
3. At cached decoding time, continue the absolute position offset from the prefix. Long-context extension still needs validation; relative structure alone does not guarantee extrapolation.

#### Explain LoRA and calculate its trainable parameter count.

LoRA represents a weight update as the product of two thin matrices while leaving the original weight frozen.

1. For W shaped (d_out,d_in), use delta_W=(alpha/r)*B@A with A:(r,d_in) and B:(d_out,r).
2. Train A and B, usually initializing one factor to zero so the initial update is zero without making both gradients permanently zero.
3. The trainable count is r*(d_in+d_out), versus d_in*d_out for the full matrix. Include all targeted matrices when estimating the whole model.

#### Why use AdamW, warmup and gradient clipping?

They address different training problems: adaptive update scale, early-step instability and unusually large gradients.

1. Adam maintains first and second moment estimates, with bias correction after zero initialization.
2. AdamW applies decoupled weight decay rather than treating decay exactly as another adaptively scaled loss gradient.
3. Warm up learning rate over initial steps and clip the global gradient norm when appropriate. Inspect logs and ablations; these are tuning tools, not correctness fixes.

### Understand why the approach works

#### Derive the low-rank update and count what is trained

For a frozen base matrix $W\in\mathbb R^{d_{\rm out}\times d_{\rm in}}$, LoRA uses

$$
W'=W+\frac\alpha r BA,\qquad
A\in\mathbb R^{r\times d_{\rm in}},\quad B\in\mathbb R^{d_{\rm out}\times r}.
$$

Only $A$ and $B$ need optimizer updates for this adaptation. Their parameter count is $r(d_{\rm in}+d_{\rm out})$, compared with $d_{\rm in}d_{\rm out}$ base weights. A $4096\times4096$ matrix at rank eight has $65536$ adapter parameters versus $16777216$ full weights.

A common initialization sets one factor to zero and the other random, so the initial update is zero while learning can start. If $G$ is the derivative with respect to $BA$, then $\partial L/\partial B=GA^\top$ and $\partial L/\partial A=B^\top G$. Setting both factors to zero makes both derivatives zero; setting only $B=0$ still permits a first update to $B$ when $A$ is nonzero.

Sum adapters over all targeted matrices, include scaling and any trainable biases, and distinguish trainable-state savings from activation memory. The frozen base still participates in the forward computation. Merging a compatible adapter into a dense layer can remove a separate adapter path at inference; quantized storage and multiple active adapters complicate that operation. Compare adaptation quality and inference constraints rather than assuming the smallest rank is always best.

### Work a small example

In “The dog chased the ball because it moved,” a pronoun's query may match an object's key while its value carries object attributes. The three vectors serve different jobs even though they start from the same token state.

### Edge cases and common mistakes

Calling Q, K and V three different input sequences: self-attention projects the same input; cross-attention uses a different source for K and V.

### Check your reasoning on related cases

**Why divide attention logits by the square root of the head dimension?** With d_k=64, the unscaled score has standard deviation 8 under these assumptions. Scaling reduces it to 1; dividing by 64 would make the scores unnecessarily small.

Could learned temperature or QK normalization change this calculation? Yes; state how the effective score scale is controlled.

**Trace every shape in multi-head self-attention.** For B=2, T=3, H=4, d_h=8, each score tensor has shape (2,4,3,3); concatenating produces (2,3,32).

What changes for cross-attention? The score matrix becomes (B,H,T_query,T_key), which can be rectangular.

**Why do Transformers need positional information, and what does RoPE do?** For a coordinate pair (1,0), a 90-degree rotation gives (0,1), preserving length. Applying the same rotation to both q and k leaves their mutual dot product unchanged.

What is cached with RoPE? Typically already-rotated keys and ordinary values; the exact representation depends on the implementation.

**Explain LoRA and calculate its trainable parameter count.** A 4096-by-4096 matrix with r=8 has 65536 adapter parameters instead of 16777216 full parameters, excluding biases.

Can the adapter be merged? Usually yes for a compatible linear layer; quantized storage and multiple adapters need extra care.
