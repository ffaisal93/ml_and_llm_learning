## Worked solution

1. Define label windows for click-through-rate analysis. The numerator and denominator must refer to the same eligible exposures and mature observation windows.
2. Define an impression, a valid click, attribution rules, deduplication and the outcome window.
3. Join outcomes to exposures without multiplying rows, then aggregate clicks and impressions by the intended category.
4. Report counts and uncertainty; distinguish unobserved outcomes from confirmed non-clicks when logs are incomplete.
5. Fit a baseline on messy tabular data without leakage. Build a trustworthy split and simple baseline before sophisticated feature engineering.
6. Define the target, prediction time and unit of independence, then split by time or group if required.
7. Fit imputation, scaling and category encodings on training folds only. Start with a dummy baseline and a regularized linear or small tree model.
8. Evaluate slices, missingness patterns and calibration; document features unavailable at decision time and remove them.

### Small example / sanity check

A category with 2 clicks from 2 impressions has CTR 100% but far more uncertainty than one with 10000 clicks from 20000 impressions.

### Common trap

Averaging per-user CTRs when the requested metric is total clicks divided by total impressions.
