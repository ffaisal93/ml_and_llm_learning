## Question

<pre class="question-text" style="white-space:pre-wrap;font-family:inherit;line-height:1.55">What are L1 and L2 regularization? What are the differences between them?</pre>

## Worked solution

1. Compare L1 and L2 regularization. Both penalize large coefficients, but their geometry produces different coefficient patterns.
2. Add lambda*sum(abs(w)) for L1 or a stated lambda-scaled squared norm for L2.
3. L1 can produce exact zeros with suitable optimization, aiding sparse selection; L2 usually shrinks coefficients smoothly and stabilizes correlated predictors.
4. Standardize features using training statistics, tune lambda on validation data and state whether the intercept is penalized.

### Small example / sanity check

With two almost identical predictors, L1 may choose one unstably while L2 spreads weight across both.

### Common trap

Interpreting a selected correlated feature as uniquely causal, or comparing unscaled coefficients across different units.
