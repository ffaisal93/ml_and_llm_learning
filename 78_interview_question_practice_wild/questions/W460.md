## Question

What actions do you usually take when facing a major challenge? Please provide an example.

## Worked solution

### Reduce uncertainty and protect the next decision

“When I face a major challenge, I first state the outcome and immediate risk, separate known facts from assumptions, and identify the smallest action that reduces the most consequential uncertainty. I ask the right people for help early, assign owners and checkpoints, and communicate scope/date choices as evidence changes. After mitigation, I check the result and fix the process that allowed the problem.” This is a proposed approach; support it with a real example.

**Illustrative example:** a release is blocked by a sudden p99 latency regression. I own serving, while another team owns a provider. I first pause rollout and preserve the old version, then compare request mix and traces. Queueing, not model compute, dominates after a retry-policy change. I reproduce the amplification under a bounded load, disable layered retries, add deadline/admission limits and coordinate a provider-specific circuit breaker with its owner. I send stakeholders a short status with what is known, user impact and the next checkpoint, rather than a confident untested root cause.

Validation uses matched workload latency/error rates and a failure drill; actual numbers must come from your incident, not this example. The learning is to budget retries end-to-end and test overload before rollout. The method balances urgent mitigation with evidence and collaboration. Persisting alone without communicating, or rewriting the largest component before locating the failing stage, would make the challenge larger.
