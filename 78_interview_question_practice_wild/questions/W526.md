## Question

Self-introduction and Project Experience

Please provide a detailed introduction of the project you are currently working on. Include the project's objectives, your role in it, challenges faced, and how you resolved those challenges. How do you think your project experience and skills can be transferred to the field of economic consulting?

Motivation and Interest

Please explain why you are interested in economic consulting. What motivates you to choose this field? What do you think is the most important skill for a successful economic consultant?

Example

### Input

Your role: Project Manager
Project Challenges: Integrating data from diverse systems
Skills: Analytical thinking, problem-solving
Interest: Economic consulting since college

## Worked solution

### Explain the project and the transfer separately

The prompt's “project manager, diverse systems, analytical thinking” fields are example inputs, not your biography. Use a real current/recent project and distinguish your personal work from the team's. Economic consulting values defensible analysis and clear communication; generic enthusiasm for numbers is not enough.

### Project answer structure

State the client's/user's decision, then the project objective. Describe the data, your role and the hardest analytical or coordination issue. For a genuine data-integration project, explain how you defined keys, reconciled conflicting units/time windows, tracked lineage and verified output against a trusted total. Give the actual result and a limitation, not an invented percentage improvement.

A study phrasing is: “The project supports [decision] by combining [data sources]. I was responsible for [specific work]. We found [real mismatch/challenge], so I [action and validation]. The result was [measured outcome]. One limitation is [remaining uncertainty], which I would address through [next check].”

### Translate the skills into consulting work

Data cleaning transfers to constructing reliable datasets from client and public records. Model comparison transfers to testing alternative explanations. Sensitivity analysis transfers to showing which assumptions drive a conclusion. Written documentation and reproducible code let another analyst review the analysis. Managing deadlines and explaining discrepancies helps a consulting team communicate with clients and specialists.

Crucially, prediction is not causation. A project that predicts demand does not by itself show the causal effect of a price change. Explain how a consulting question might require a credible counterfactual, identification strategy and robustness checks rather than merely a more accurate predictor.

### Motivation and the most important skill

Give your genuine reason for wanting applied economic problem-solving in this setting—such as connecting data to a contested business question and explaining uncertainty to decision-makers. A defensible choice for the most important skill is **structured, evidence-based reasoning**: framing the right question, checking assumptions and communicating what the evidence can and cannot support. Quantitative methods, domain knowledge and clear writing enable that skill; none eliminates the need for judgment.

Prepare follow-ups about an alternative you rejected, a disagreement you resolved and a conclusion that changed after validation. Your exact personal example is a missing input, so this page supplies the reasoning and response structure rather than pretending to know your project history.
