## Worked solution

1. Why can regression fit independent Gaussian noise? Training fit measures how well a model projects onto its fitted feature space, not whether a real predictive relationship exists.
2. Assume a fixed rank-r design with an intercept and an independent isotropic Gaussian response.
3. After removing the mean, projection onto r-1 fitted directions captures expected fraction (r-1)/(n-1) of centered response energy.
4. Thus expected training R² is (r-1)/(n-1) under these assumptions; evaluate on independent data to reveal lack of predictive signal.

### Small example / sanity check

With n=101 and r=21 including the intercept, expected training R² is 20/100=0.2 despite independence.

### Common trap

Applying this formula to arbitrary dependent designs, no-intercept conventions or held-out R² without re-deriving assumptions.
