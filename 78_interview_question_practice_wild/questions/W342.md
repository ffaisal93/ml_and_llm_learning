## Question

<pre class="question-text" style="white-space:pre-wrap;font-family:inherit;line-height:1.55">You are given transactions(transaction_id INT, vendor_id INT, amount_charged INT, transaction_type VARCHAR, refund_transaction_id INT NULL, timestamp DATETIME) where transaction_type is PURCHASE or REFUND (a REFUND&#x27;s refund_transaction_id points to the original purchase transaction_id) and vendors(vendor_id INT, city VARCHAR, state VARCHAR, country VARCHAR).

Write a SQL query to: compute each vendor&#x27;s net total charged amount in the last 2 years (relative to CURRENT_DATE), where PURCHASE is positive and REFUND is negative; return the Top 3 vendors by net total amount (descending).

Suggested output columns: vendor_id, net_amount.

Assumptions/constraints
Refund rows are counted based on the refund timestamp and must be within the 2-year window to be included.
amount_charged is a positive integer.
transaction_id is unique.
Example

Current date assumed 2025-04-01. Output: vendor_id=3 net_amount=500; vendor_id=2 net_amount=300; vendor_id=1 net_amount=150 (101 and 103 cancel out, +102 = 150).</pre>

## Worked solution

1. Find the top three vendors by net charge over the last two years. Aggregate at the intended vendor grain before ranking, with refunds and time boundaries defined.
2. Filter transactions using a reproducible as-of timestamp and a half-open date interval.
3. Normalize charge/refund signs or subtract refund amounts according to the schema, then group by vendor id.
4. Sort totals descending with an explicit tie rule; use ROW_NUMBER for exactly three rows or DENSE_RANK when tied ranks should be included.

### Small example / sanity check

A vendor with charges 100 and refunds 30 has net charge 70, not gross charge 100.

### Common trap

Joining a one-to-many vendor table before aggregation and multiplying transaction totals.
