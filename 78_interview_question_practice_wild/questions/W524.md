## Question

<pre class="question-text" style="white-space:pre-wrap;font-family:inherit;line-height:1.55">A set of nine general interview-preparation questions for job candidates (new-grad / intern leaning). Several questions reference the job description (JD) and the Dallas job market, so answers are expected to be tailored to the specific role and location.

1. Why should we hire you?

Please explain in detail your skills and experiences and how they align with the requirements of the candidate we are looking for. Make sure to provide specific examples of your past achievements and how you can add value to this position by referring to the job description (JD).

2. Describe a course or project you took part in and how it relates to this position.

Please introduce a course you took or a project you participated in, and how it prepared you for this position. Provide specific details, such as the objectives of the project, your particular involvement, and the outcomes.

3. Tell me about a failure you&#x27;ve experienced and how you dealt with it.

Please share a failure experience from your career or academic life, describe how you handled and faced this failure, and what lessons you learned from it.

4. What do you know about the Dallas job market?

Please introduce your understanding of the Dallas job market and your enthusiasm for working in this area. You might need to conduct some market research in advance to provide some data or facts supporting your viewpoint.

5. If we hire you, what kind of work would you like to do?

Please state the main work content you expect to engage in and the experiences and growth you hope to gain from this position. Elaborate based on the job description when answering.

6. When does your summer internship start and end?

Please provide the specific timeline for your summer internship and explain whether you can begin employment with our company on time.

7. When do you graduate, and are you planning to advance your education or enter the industry?

Please specify your graduation date and whether your future plan is to pursue further studies or enter the workforce.

8. What school are you currently attending?

Please provide the name of the school you are currently attending.

9. What skills do you have?

Please list your main skills, especially those related to the applied position, and provide relevant experience or certificates to support your statements.

## Worked solution

1. Present your background in ninety seconds. Connect your real experience to the role through a small number of relevant capabilities and outcomes.
2. Start with your current focus and the kinds of problems you solve.
3. Choose one or two concrete projects, naming your role, difficult decision and measured result.
4. Finish with why the target work fits your interests and what you want to contribute, without inventing credentials or metrics.

### Apply the exact contract

Answer the specific question directly using truthful details: motivation connects your interests to the work; a job-change answer explains the decision without disparaging others; a timeline answer states real deadlines and availability. For active contacts, share only what you are comfortable disclosing and never invent offers, company names or commitments. For a news-based motivation prompt, verify the relevant fact before using it; no specific current headline is supplied here.

### Small example / sanity check

An MLE introduction can connect data-quality work, reliable serving and one measured deployment result instead of listing every library used.

### Common trap

Reciting the entire resume chronologically or making claims you cannot defend under follow-up.
